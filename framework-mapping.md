 IAM Audit Framework Mapping

The control weaknesses identified during the simulated IAM audit were mapped to relevant controls and outcomes from:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A

The purpose of this mapping is to demonstrate how the identified IAM risks relate to recognised cybersecurity control frameworks.

NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0

F-01 – Incomplete MFA Enforcement 
Category - PR.AA – Identity Management, Authentication, and Access Control
PR.AA-03
MFA was not enforced for all remotely accessible accounts, weakening authentication controls.


F-02 – Inadequate User & Contractor Offboarding
Category - PR.AA – Identity Management, Authentication, and Access Control
PR.AA-01 and PR.AA-05
User identities, credentials and access permissions were not removed promptly after employment or contractor engagements ended.


F-03 – Inadequate Periodic Access Reviews
Category - PR.AA – Identity Management, Authentication, and Access Control
PR.AA-05
Access permissions were not consistently reviewed as required, increasing the risk of users retaining unnecessary or excessive access.


F-04 – Inadequate Governance of Shared Privileged Account
Category - PR.AA – Identity Management, Authentication, and Access Control
PR.AA-01 and PR.AA-05
The shared Global Administrator account weakens individual identity accountability and lacks sufficient controls over highly privileged access.


F-05 – Inadequate Service Account Governance
Category - PR.AA – Identity Management, Authentication, and Access Control
PR.AA-01 and PR.AA-05
Service identities lacked documented ownership and appropriate governance, while several accounts held privileged permissions without sufficient management oversight.



ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A

F-01 – Incomplete MFA Enforcement 
Category- Technological Controls
A.8.5 – Secure Authentication
MFA was not enforced for all remotely accessible accounts, weakening the organisation's authentication controls.


F-02 – Inadequate User & Contractor Offboarding
Category- A.5 – Organisational Controls
A.5.16 – Identity Management, A.5.18 – Access Rights
User identities and access rights were not consistently deactivated or revoked when employment or contractor engagements ended.

F-03 – Inadequate Periodic Access Reviews
Category - A.5 – Organisational Controls
A.5.18 – Access Rights
Access rights were not reviewed at the required frequency, increasing the risk that unnecessary permissions remain active.


F-04 – Inadequate Governance of Shared Privileged Account
Category -  A.5 – Organisational Controls and A.8 – Technological Controls
A.5.16 – Identity Management and A.8.2 – Privileged Access Rights
The shared Global Administrator account weakens individual identity accountability and lacks adequate governance over highly privileged access.


F-05 – Inadequate Service Account Governance
Category - A.5 – Organisational Controls and A.8 – Technological Controls
A.5.16 – Identity Management; A.8.2 – Privileged Access Rights
Service accounts lacked documented ownership and identity governance, while several accounts also possessed privileged permissions.
