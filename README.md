## IAM-Security-Audit
Simulated Identity and Access Management security audit assessing IAM controls, risks, and remediation recommendations.

## Overview

This project presents a simulated internal cybersecurity audit of Identity and Access Management (IAM) controls within a fictional technology organisation, NovaTech Solutions.

The assessment was conducted to evaluate whether identity and access controls are appropriately designed and operating effectively to reduce the risk of unauthorised access to corporate systems and sensitive information.

## Organisation Background

NovaTech Solutions is a fictional UAE-based technology services company with approximately 180 employees. The organisation provides cloud-based business applications to corporate clients and operates in a hybrid working environment.

Its technology environment includes:

Microsoft 365
Microsoft Entra ID
AWS cloud services
Windows 11 endpoints
SaaS applications
VPN-based remote administrative access

The organisation processes customer information, employee records, financial information, contracts, and internal business data.

Following rapid organisational growth, management initiated an internal review to determine whether IAM processes and controls had matured sufficiently to support the organisation's expanded workforce and technology environment.

## Audit Objective

The objective of the assessment is to evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of key IAM controls and identify weaknesses that could result in unauthorised or inappropriate access to NovaTech's systems and information.



## Disclaimer

NovaTech Solutions is a fictional organisation created solely for this cybersecurity portfolio project. All systems, users, evidence, observations, and findings used in this assessment are simulated.
