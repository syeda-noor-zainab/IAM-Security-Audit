Audit Scope and Methodology

Audit Objective

The objective of this assessment is to evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of Identity and Access Management (IAM) controls within NovaTech Solutions and identify control weaknesses that could increase the risk of unauthorised or inappropriate access.

Scope

The assessment covers the following IAM control areas:
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
User account provisioning and deprovisioning
Joiner-Mover-Leaver (JML) processes
Contractor access management
Periodic access reviews
Privileged access management
Service account management
Access approval processes
Privileged activity logging

Technical vulnerability testing, penetration testing, network security and application security are outside the scope of this assessment.

Methodology

The audit was performed using a simulated evidence-based control assessment. Evidence was reviewed to determine whether IAM controls were appropriately designed and whether they were operating as intended.

The assessment involved:

1. Reviewing the organisation's simulated Access Control Policy and defined IAM requirements.
2. Examining simulated user, contractor, administrator and service account records.
3. Reviewing samples of access requests, account termination records and privileged administrative activity.
4. Comparing observed practices against defined organisational requirements.
5. Identifying control exceptions and evaluating the associated security risk.
6. Developing remediation recommendations for identified control weaknesses.

Where testing demonstrated that a control was operating as expected, no audit finding was raised.

## Assessment Approach

Each potential control weakness was evaluated using four core elements:

Criteria — what should be happening.
Condition— what was observed during testing.
Risk— the potential consequence of the control weakness.
Recommendation — actions management should take to address the weakness.

Findings were assigned a severity rating of Critical, High, Medium or Low based on the potential likelihood and impact of the identified risk.
